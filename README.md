 **Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis – 2022**
1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the annual sales performance of Vrinda Store (2022) to identify key business insights related to sales trends, customer demographics, channels, and top-performing states.

The goal of this analysis is to help the business understand:

Which months generated the highest revenue

Which sales channels perform best

Gender-wise and age-wise customer behavior

Top contributing states

Order delivery performance

2. Tools & Technologies Used

- Excel (Data Cleaning & Dashboard Creation)
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Visualizations
- Slicers for Interactive Filtering

3. Dashboard Features
1️⃣ Sales vs Orders (Monthly Trend)

- Displays monthly sales amount vs number of orders.
- Helps identify peak sales months.
- Useful for seasonal demand analysis.

2️⃣ Sales by Channel

Channel contribution breakdown:
- Amazon – 35.48%
- Myntra – 23.36%
- Flipkart – 21.59%
- Ajio – 6.22%
- Meesho – 4.50%
- Nalli & Others – Remaining %
👉 Insight: Amazon is the top-performing sales channel.

3️⃣ Sales: Men vs Women

- Women: 69.42%
- Men: 30.58%
👉 Insight: Women contribute significantly higher sales than men.

4️⃣ Top 5 States by Sales

- Maharashtra – 2.99M
- Karnataka – 2.65M
- Uttar Pradesh – 2.10M
- Telangana – 1.71M
- Tamil Nadu – 1.68M
👉 Insight: Maharashtra is the highest revenue-generating state.

5️⃣ Orders: Age vs Gender

- Adult women contribute the highest orders.
- Teenagers and senior citizens have lower order share.
👉 Insight: Adult women are the primary target customer segment.

6️⃣ Order Status Analysis

- Delivered – 92%
- Cancelled – 3%
- Returned – 3%
- Refunded – 2%
👉 Insight: High delivery success rate (92%) indicates strong operational efficiency.

4. Key Business Insights

📈 Sales peak in early months and slightly decline toward year-end.
🛒 Amazon dominates as primary sales channel.
👩 Women (especially adults) are the main customer base.
🌍 Maharashtra and Karnataka drive major revenue.
🚚 Strong delivery performance (92% delivered rate).

5. Business Recommendation

- Focus marketing efforts on women (adult segment).
- Strengthen Amazon partnership.
- Expand operations in Maharashtra & Karnataka.
- Improve cancellation and return rate further.
